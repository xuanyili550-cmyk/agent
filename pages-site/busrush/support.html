Support

Email jasperabundant@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 48 hours.

Why can’t this passenger board?

Only the passenger at the front of the queue can move, and only onto a bus of the same colour standing at the kerb. Anyone else has to wait, or be parked in a bay.

Is the level impossible?

No. Every level is run through a solver before it ships, using the bay count free players have, and is rejected unless it can be proved clearable. If you are stuck, tap Undo or Restart — the route exists.

What is “Next to arrive”?

The bus that will take the stand once a current one fills and leaves. Use it to decide who is worth parking in a bay.

I’m colour blind

Every colour also carries a distinct shape — circle, triangle, square and so on — on both passengers and buses, so you can match by shape alone.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open your Apple Account subscriptions, tap Bus Rush, then Cancel Subscription. Also in the app: Settings → Manage or Cancel Subscription.

I paid but Complete isn’t showing

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you purchased with.

Does it work offline?

Yes, completely.
