Imposter Night

One phone. Everybody gets a word — except the faker.

Imposter Night is a privacy-first iOS app. Everything runs on your device — no account, no tracking, no servers, no advertising. It works completely offline.

How it works

Pass the phone around. Everyone sees the same secret word, except the imposter, who gets a close relative of it. Take turns saying one word about yours, then vote. The crew wins by finding the faker; the faker wins by surviving — or by guessing the real word on the way out.

The whole game runs on one device with no connection of any kind. The word pairs are hand-written so that one honest clue fits both sides and the second starts to hurt.

Imposter Night Plus

The app is free to download. Free play includes two word packs and tables of up to six players with one imposter. Pro unlocks all ten packs, tables up to twenty, two imposters, custom word pairs you write yourself, and the discussion timer. There is no advertising in the app whether or not you subscribe.
