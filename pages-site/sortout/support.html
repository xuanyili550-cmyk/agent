How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. Open your Apple Account subscriptions, tap Sort Out, then tap Cancel Subscription. You can also reach that screen from inside the app: Settings → Manage or Cancel Subscription.

I paid but Pro isn’t showing

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you purchased with.

I’m stuck on a level

Every level in Sort Out is checked by a solver before it is shown to you — none of them are dead ends. Use the Hint button for the next move from that same solver, or Restart to reset the board. Pro adds an extra spare tube you can drop into any level.

Does it work offline?

Yes, completely. There is no network code in the app apart from Apple’s purchase handling.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

What is free and what is paid?

Levels 1–15, three undos and one hint per level, and one colour theme are free. Pro unlocks every level, unlimited undo and hints, an extra spare tube, and six themes. There is no advertising either way.
