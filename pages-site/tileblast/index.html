Tile Blast

Join matching tiles — if the path can get there.

Tile Blast is a privacy-first iOS puzzle game. Everything runs on your device — no account, no tracking, no servers, no advertising. It works completely offline.

How it plays

Tap two tiles with the same symbol. They clear if a path can run between them through empty space, turning at most twice. Every stage is built by filling an empty board in reverse removal order, so a winning line always exists before you see the first tile.

Tile Blast Unlimited

The app is free to download. Free play covers the first eight stages, with three undos, one hint and one shuffle per stage. Pro unlocks every stage — boards grow to 10×12 with sixteen symbols — plus unlimited undo, hints and shuffles. There is no advertising in the app whether or not you subscribe.
