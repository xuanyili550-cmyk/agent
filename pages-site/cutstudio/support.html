Support

Email jasperabundant@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 48 hours.

Does it upload my videos anywhere?

No. Trimming, joining and exporting all run on your iPhone using Apple's video framework. There is no server and no network code in the app beyond Apple's own purchase handling. It works in Airplane Mode.

Why doesn't it ask for photo access?

Because it doesn't need it. The system photo picker runs outside the app and hands over only the clips you choose, and the finished video goes to the share sheet rather than being written into your library. So there is nothing to ask permission for.

Is the free export really watermarked?

Yes, visibly. It is not a nag or a promise — the watermark is composited into the video, and Pro exports are rendered without that layer. If you see a watermark on a Pro export, use Restore Purchases in Settings and it will re-export clean.

What happens to my projects if I cancel?

They stay on the device. You can still open and export them; exports simply go back to 720p with the watermark.

Does it need an internet connection?

Never. Everything runs on the device. It works in Airplane Mode.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. Open your Apple Account subscriptions, tap Cut Studio, then tap Cancel Subscription. You can also reach that screen from inside the app: Settings → Manage or Cancel Subscription.

I paid but Pro isn’t showing

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you purchased with.

Does it work offline?

Yes, completely. There is no network code in the app apart from Apple’s purchase handling.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
