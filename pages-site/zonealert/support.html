Is this an emergency service?

No. Zone Alert is not an emergency service and must not be relied on as one. It is a convenience layer over public feeds that can be delayed or incomplete. Always follow official warnings and local emergency broadcasts. In an emergency in the United States, call 911.

Where does the data come from?

Two free public sources: the US National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) for weather warnings, and the US Geological Survey (earthquake.usgs.gov) for earthquakes. We run no server of our own and add nothing to what they publish.

Does it cover countries outside the United States?

No. Both feeds are United States government sources, so coverage is the United States and its territories.

Why do I not get a notification?

Notifications need permission, and iOS decides when a background app is allowed to refresh. Open the app to force a refresh. Notifications on alert matches are a Pro feature.

What do you do with my location?

The centre point of each watch area is sent to api.weather.gov once, to look up the forecast zone for that spot. Nothing identifying you is attached, and we never receive it ourselves — we have no server. The app can be used entirely with areas you place by hand, without granting location access at all.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. Open your Apple Account subscriptions, tap Zone Alert, then tap Cancel Subscription. You can also reach that screen from inside the app: Settings → Manage or Cancel Subscription.

I paid but Pro isn’t showing

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you purchased with.

Does it work offline?

Partly. Everything already downloaded stays readable with no connection, so you can open the app on a plane and still read the alerts and their full text. Fetching new warnings needs an internet connection, because the warnings come from the National Weather Service and the US Geological Survey rather than from inside the app. When it cannot refresh, the app tells you so and shows the time of the last successful update — it never shows an old warning as if it were current.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
