Zone Alert

Watch the places you care about for official hazard warnings. Not an emergency service.

Zone Alert is a privacy-first iOS app. No account, no tracking, no advertising, and no server of ours anywhere. Alerts you have already downloaded stay readable without a connection, but new ones need one.

How it works

Zone Alert is not an emergency service and must not be relied on as one. It shows warnings published by the US National Weather Service and earthquake records published by the US Geological Survey. It can only show them when your iPhone has a working internet connection and iOS has allowed the app to refresh. Alerts can be delayed, missed entirely, or withdrawn by their publisher after you see them here. Always follow local emergency broadcasts, official warnings, and the instructions of the authorities themselves. In an emergency in the United States, call 911.

You draw a circle on a map around a place that matters to you — home, a relative, a route you drive. The app fetches the active warnings, matches them against your circles on the device, and lists whatever applies. Tap an alert to read the full official text and see the area on the map.

When a reading cannot be refreshed, the app says so and shows the time of the last successful update. It never presents an old or expired warning as if it were current.

Zone Alert Pro

The free version watches one area and shows matching alerts inside the app.

Zone Alert Pro watches as many areas as you like, sends a notification when a new alert matches one of them, lets you filter by alert type, and keeps the history.

There is no advertising in this app whether or not you subscribe, and there is nothing else to buy.
