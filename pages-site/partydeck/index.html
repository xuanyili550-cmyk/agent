Party Deck

One phone, a room full of people, 572 cards.

Party Deck is a privacy-first iOS app. Everything runs on your device — no account, no tracking, no servers, no advertising. It works completely offline.

How it works

Pick a deck, put the names in, and pass the phone. Each card is read out loud; the group points, splits or argues depending on the deck, and the app keeps score and whose turn it is.

Eight decks with four different mechanics: point at one person, commit to one of two sides, put a finger down, or just talk. Every card is hand-written — none of it is generated from a template.

Party Deck Complete

The app is free to download. Free play includes two decks (161 cards), tables of up to six players, and no timer or team mode. Pro unlocks all eight decks (572 cards), tables up to twenty, custom decks you write yourself, the party timer and team mode.

No advertising, ever — paid or free. And the purchase button states the exact amount that will be charged, with a one-tap link to Apple’s subscription management from both the paywall and Settings.
