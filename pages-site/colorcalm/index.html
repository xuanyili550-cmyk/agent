Color Calm

Paint-by-number that draws its own line art. No downloads, no ads, no account.

Color Calm is a privacy-first iOS app. No account, no tracking, no advertising, and no server of ours anywhere. It works completely offline.

How it works

Pick a design and fill it in. Tap a colour, then tap any region carrying that number; the wrong colour is refused, the right one fills with a soft animation. Your progress is kept as you go.

Nothing is downloaded, because nothing is a picture file. Every design — mandalas built from repeating symmetric arcs, and geometric tilings — is drawn by the app from its own number, so the same number gives the same artwork on every device and after a reinstall. Designs run from 34 to 138 regions and four to seven colours.

Color Calm Unlimited

The free version includes a starter set of designs with one palette, and no export.

Color Calm Unlimited opens every design — and because they are generated rather than shipped, there is no end to them — along with all palettes and image export.

There is no advertising in this app whether or not you subscribe, and there is nothing else to buy.
