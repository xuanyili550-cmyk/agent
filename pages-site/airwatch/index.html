Air Watch

EPA air quality for the places you care about, with alerts you set yourself.

Air Watch is a privacy-first iOS app. No account, no tracking, no advertising, and no server of ours anywhere. Readings you have already fetched stay readable without a connection, but new ones need one.

How it works

Pin a place — home, work, a relative, anywhere in the United States — and Air Watch shows the EPA AirNow air quality index for the reporting area covering it: the number, the band it falls in, the EPA's own guidance for that band, and which pollutant is leading.

Air Watch never shows a reading it did not receive. Every screen carries the time of the last successful refresh. A reading more than three hours old is labelled as old, one more than twelve hours old is not shown at all, and when the app cannot reach AirNow it says so instead of leaving yesterday's number sitting there looking current.

It is a convenience layer over a public feed. Readings can be delayed, missing for an area, or revised after you see them, and it is not a substitute for official health guidance.

Air Watch Plus

The free version covers one pinned place and shows the reading now, with no alerts and no chart.

Air Watch Plus pins as many places as you like, notifies you when the index at a place goes above a number you pick, and keeps the last 24 hours of readings as a chart.

An alert fires once when the index crosses your number and can fire again only after it has dropped back below it — so a three-day wildfire is one notification, not seventy.

There is no advertising in this app whether or not you subscribe, and there is nothing else to buy.
