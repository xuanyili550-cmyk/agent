Do I have to give it my location?

No. Every feature works with places you add by typing a city name or a ZIP code, resolved by Apple's on-device geocoder. Location is requested only if you tap “Use My Location”, and never at launch.

Which countries does it cover?

The United States and its territories. AirNow is a US EPA programme, so there is no data for other countries.

Why does a place say it has no reading?

AirNow publishes by reporting area, and some places are not inside one, or an area occasionally publishes nothing for an hour. Air Watch says so rather than showing a number it does not have — an index of zero on this scale means “Good”, which would be the worst possible thing to invent.

Why did I not get an alert?

Alerts are a Plus feature and need notification permission. Beyond that, iOS decides when a background app is allowed to refresh, so an alert can arrive late or wait until you next open the app. Air Watch cannot make iOS wake it, and it does not run a server that could push to you.

Is this an emergency or medical service?

No. It is a convenience layer over the EPA's public feed and is not a substitute for official health guidance or medical advice.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. Open your Apple Account subscriptions, tap Air Watch, then tap Cancel Subscription. You can also reach that screen from inside the app: Settings → Manage or Cancel Subscription.

I paid but Plus isn’t showing

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you purchased with.

Does it work offline?

Partly. Readings already fetched stay readable with no connection, and so does everything else in the app. Getting a new reading needs an internet connection, because air quality data comes from the EPA's AirNow service rather than from inside the app. When it cannot refresh, Air Watch says so and shows the time of the last successful refresh — it never presents an old reading as if it were current.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
