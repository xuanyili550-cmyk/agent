Quiz Rush

Six hundred hand-written trivia questions against the clock. No ads, no account.

Quiz Rush is a privacy-first iOS app. Everything runs on your device — no account, no tracking, no servers, no advertising. It works completely offline.

How it works

Pick a category and answer as many as you can before the clock runs out. A run of correct answers builds a streak, and the streak is what moves your score.

All 600 questions are written and checked by hand across ten categories. Nothing is generated, so a question always has exactly one defensible answer.

Quiz Rush Full Access

Free play includes three complete categories — Geography, Food & Drink and Everyday Objects, 180 questions in total — with three lives per run. They are whole categories, not trial slices.

Quiz Rush Full Access opens all ten categories (600 questions), removes the life limit so only the clock can stop you, shows the explanation after every question, keeps a review list of what you got wrong and why, tracks a mastery level and lifetime accuracy per category, and adds Mixed Rush, which draws from all ten categories at once.

There is no advertising in this app whether or not you subscribe, and there is nothing else to buy. If a subscription lapses, the high scores you already set are kept.
