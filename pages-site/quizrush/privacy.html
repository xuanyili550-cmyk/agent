Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Short version

Quiz Rush collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The app works fully in Airplane Mode.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifier, and no third-party networking code.

What stays on your device

Your scores, streaks and preferences are stored locally on your iPhone using Apple’s standard on-device storage. This never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We never see or receive your name, email address, or payment details. Apple’s handling of that information is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children

Quiz Rush is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
