Data accuracy

Tide predictions are computed locally from NOAA CO-OPS harmonic constants using the standard harmonic method. Sun, moon, and solunar values use public-domain astronomy algorithms. Tide and astronomy are physical phenomena; predicted tide turns land within a few minutes of NOAA's own published predictions, but actual conditions vary with weather, atmospheric pressure, and currents. Do not use this app as the sole basis for navigation decisions.
