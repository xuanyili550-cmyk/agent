My shots keep falling short

Raise the power slider. Full power is calibrated to reach the castle from the trebuchet, so if a shot lands short there is room left on the slider.

What does the dashed line mean?

It is the collapse line. Only blocks that start above it count toward the target percentage, and they count once they end up below it.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open your Apple Account subscriptions, tap Castle Crush, then Cancel Subscription. Also in the app: Settings → Manage or Cancel Subscription.

I paid but Unlimited isn’t showing

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you purchased with.

Does it work offline?

Yes, completely.

What is free and what is paid?

Levels 1–12 with the Boulder are free. Pro unlocks every level, three more projectile types, the trajectory guide and slow motion. No advertising either way.
