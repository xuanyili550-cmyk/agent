Phrase a Day

240 travel Spanish phrases, spaced repetition, and your device's own voice. No ads, no account.

Phrase a Day is a privacy-first iOS app. No account, no tracking, no advertising, and no server of ours anywhere. It works completely offline.

How it works

Every day the app shows you what is due. You see the English, try to produce the Spanish, then reveal the answer and say how well you knew it. That answer decides when the phrase comes back — something you knew instantly returns in weeks, something you forgot returns tomorrow.

All 240 phrases are written out and shipped inside the app across twelve scenes, from the airport to saying goodnight. Nothing is generated, nothing is downloaded, and each phrase carries a short note about when to use it or what it literally says.

The Spanish is neutral Latin American and addresses strangers as usted, which is the safer default across the region.

Phrase a Day Pro

Free covers three of the twelve scenes — Greetings & Basics, Airport & Arrival and Getting Around, 60 of the 240 phrases — and reviews up to 10 phrases a day. They are whole scenes, not trial slices.

Phrase a Day Pro opens all twelve scenes and all 240 phrases, raises the daily session to 40, and counts your streak with two freezes a month for the days you miss.

There is no advertising in this app whether or not you subscribe, and there is nothing else to buy — no gems, no hearts, no lesson unlocks. If a subscription lapses, everything you have already learned is kept.
