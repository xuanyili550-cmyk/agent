Support

Email jasperabundant@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 48 hours.

I’m stuck and the hint didn’t help

Every riddle has a full reveal behind the hint, which spells the answer out rather than nudging. Reveals are a Pro feature.

Do I need to rotate or shake my phone?

Three riddles read the device that way, and all three also accept a two-finger twist on screen, so nothing is unsolvable if you would rather not move the phone. A test enforces that.

Does the app need any permissions?

None at all. It asks for nothing — no camera, microphone, location, photos, contacts or notifications.

Is it really 42?

Yes, numbered 1 to 42 with no gaps. A test asserts the level list is exactly that, and that no two riddles share a prompt, hint, reveal or title.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. Open your Apple Account subscriptions, tap Riddle Box, then tap Cancel Subscription. You can also reach that screen from inside the app: Settings → Manage or Cancel Subscription.

I paid but Full Access isn’t showing

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you purchased with.

Does it work offline?

Yes, completely. There is no network code in the app apart from Apple’s purchase handling.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
