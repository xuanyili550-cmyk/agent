Ink Try

See a tattoo design on your own skin before it's permanent. Everything on device, nothing uploaded.

Ink Try is a privacy-first iOS app. No account, no tracking, no advertising, and no server of ours anywhere. It works completely offline.

How it works

Take or choose a photo of an arm, shoulder or back, place a design on it, then move, rotate, scale and warp it so it follows the limb instead of sitting on top like a sticker. The ink is blended into the photo — skin tone and shading show through — and you can tune its opacity or age it to see how it might look years on.

Every design is drawn by the app from its own number: mandalas, armbands, curvework, geometry, and script in a system typeface. Nothing is anyone else's tattoo artwork and nothing is downloaded. Ink Try is a preview tool, not a tattoo service or medical advice, and it endorses no artist.

Photos never leave your iPhone. There is no upload, no account and no server. The finished preview goes to the share sheet, where you choose what happens to it.

Ink Try Plus

The free version includes a starter set of designs from two of the families, one ink style, fresh ink only, and exports with an Ink Try watermark across the bottom.

Ink Try Plus opens every design in every family, all ink styles, ink ageing, and exports without the watermark.

The watermark is real: a free export carries it and a Plus export does not, and the app has an automated test that renders both and checks the pixels. There is no advertising in this app whether or not you subscribe, and there is nothing else to buy — no per-design purchases.
