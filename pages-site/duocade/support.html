Do we need two phones?

No. Duo Cade is one phone, two players. There is no online mode, no pairing and no second device.

Why is the top half upside down?

Because the person at that end is looking at it from the other side of the table. Their half is drawn to face them, exactly as yours faces you.

Is either end easier to win from?

No. The two halves are point reflections of each other — the same layout, the same distances and the same timings, mirrored through the centre of the screen. The app is tested for this.

Does it need an internet connection?

Never. There is no network code in the app at all beyond Apple's own purchase handling. It works in Airplane Mode.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. Open your Apple Account subscriptions, tap Duo Cade, then tap Cancel Subscription. You can also reach that screen from inside the app: Settings → Manage or Cancel Subscription.

I paid but Unlimited isn’t showing

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you purchased with.

Does it work offline?

Yes, completely. There is no network code in the app apart from Apple’s purchase handling.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
