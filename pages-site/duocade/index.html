Duo Cade

Twelve two-player games on one phone. Sit at opposite ends and play.

Duo Cade is a privacy-first iOS app. Everything runs on your device — no account, no tracking, no servers, no advertising. It works completely offline.

How it works

Put the phone flat on the table between you and sit at opposite ends. Each player owns the half of the screen nearest to them — the top half is drawn upside down on purpose, so it faces the person holding that end.

Pick the games you want, tap Start match, and both of you tap your own half when you are ready. Win a game to take a point; first to win the match takes the set. There is nothing to learn beyond the one line of rules on each game's card.

Both halves are laid out from the same numbers and mirrored through the centre of the screen, so neither end is easier to reach or quicker to react from.

Duo Cade Unlimited

Free play includes four of the twelve games — Pong, Snap, Tug of War and Quick Maths — at best of 3.

Duo Cade Unlimited opens the other eight games (Air Hockey, Tank Duel, Sumo, Snake Duel, Penalties, Colour Match, Wire Cut and Rocket Dodge), adds best of 5, 7, 9 and 11, unlocks Tournament mode, and lets you name both players and keep a running head-to-head between them on this device.

There is no advertising in this app whether or not you subscribe, and there is nothing else to buy — no coins, no boosts, no unlock-one-game purchases.
