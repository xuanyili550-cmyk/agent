Key Tap

Piano tiles where every note is played, not sampled. Eight public-domain melodies, no ads, no account.

Key Tap is a privacy-first iOS app. No account, no tracking, no advertising, and no server of ours anywhere. It works completely offline.

How it works

Tiles fall down four lanes. Tap the black one before it reaches the bottom; miss one, or tap a blank, and the run ends. Each tile carries a real pitch, so a run plays a melody you know — and the faster you go, the faster it plays.

There are no audio files in this app. Every note is generated on your device at the moment your finger lands, which is why the delay between tap and sound is measured in single milliseconds rather than tens. The app shows you that number in Settings.

The eight melodies are all in the public domain and were entered by hand as note numbers and durations — Beethoven, Pachelbel, and traditional tunes whose composers are anonymous or have been dead for well over a century. Settings lists each one with the reason it is free to use.

Key Tap Premium

Free play includes three of the eight melodies — Ode to Joy, Twinkle Twinkle and Amazing Grace — at Steady speed, without note-perfect replay.

Key Tap Premium opens all eight melodies, adds the Brisk and Blistering speeds, and lets you play a finished run back exactly as you tapped it.

There is no advertising in this app whether or not you subscribe, and there is nothing else to buy — no continues, no lives, no per-melody unlocks. Your best scores are kept whether or not a subscription is active.
