# 构建顺序(bottom-up)
1 core/config 2 logging 3 exceptions → 4 schemas/ft →
5 dataset(校验) 6 trainer(stub/trl) 7 evaluator 8 registry 9 pipeline(编排5-8) 10 jobs →
11 routers/health 12 routers/train(依赖 jobs+pipeline+registry) → 13 main 14 run → 15 tests/Dockerfile
> 训练后端抽象成 trainer.train,默认 stub 让流程离线可测;真实训练 FT_TRAIN_BACKEND=trl(GPU)。
